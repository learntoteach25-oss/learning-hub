Employment Policies & Tutor Ethics

Learning Hub is committed to a respectful, safe, confidential and professionally accountable learning environment for tutors, learners and families.

1. Employment Policies

These principles apply to tutors and contractors engaged by Learning Hub. Individual engagement terms, scope of work, compensation, scheduling and termination arrangements are governed by the applicable written agreement.

Professional conduct

  • Treat every learner, parent, colleague and representative with dignity, fairness and respect.
  • Deliver lessons punctually and prepare appropriate, accurate and age-appropriate learning materials.
  • Communicate honestly about qualifications, experience, availability, lesson progress and learner outcomes.
  • Do not discriminate, harass, bully, intimidate, exploit or retaliate against any learner or colleague.
  • Do not promise guaranteed grades, examination results, admissions or other outcomes.

Recruitment and credentials

  • Provide truthful information about identity, qualifications, experience, references and right to work where relevant.
  • Supply requested documents through approved, secure channels and promptly disclose material changes.
  • Learning Hub may verify credentials and references where lawful and relevant to the role.

Confidentiality and boundaries

  • Keep learner records, contact details, assessment results, family information and internal business information confidential.
  • Use learner information only for authorised educational and administrative purposes.
  • Do not record, publish, copy or share lessons, images, messages or learner work without appropriate permission and a lawful basis.
  • Maintain professional tutor–learner boundaries; use approved communication channels and report concerns promptly.

Attendance, performance and concerns

  • Give reasonable notice of cancellations and follow the agreed scheduling and attendance process.
  • Accept constructive feedback and cooperate with reasonable quality reviews.
  • Report suspected misconduct, safeguarding concerns, security incidents or conflicts of interest promptly.
  • Policy breaches may lead to review, additional training, suspension of assignments or termination of engagement, consistent with the agreement and applicable law.

2. Legal Compliance & Safeguarding

Learning Hub expects everyone working on its behalf to comply with applicable laws, contractual duties and relevant professional standards in the jurisdictions where services are provided.

Core commitments

  • Privacy and data protection: collect only necessary personal information, restrict access, protect records and use appropriate retention and deletion practices.
  • Child safeguarding: prioritise learner safety, maintain appropriate boundaries, follow approved communication practices and escalate concerns through the designated contact or relevant authorities when required.
  • Intellectual property: respect copyright, licences and ownership of teaching resources; do not submit plagiarised or unlawfully copied materials.
  • Fair and honest dealings: avoid bribery, fraud, falsified documents, undisclosed commissions and misleading claims.
  • Information security: use strong account security, keep credentials private and report suspected unauthorised access or data loss immediately.
  • Conflicts of interest: disclose any situation that could affect impartiality, learner welfare or the proper performance of duties.
Important: This page states general organisational standards; it is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice, a signed employment or contractor agreement, a safeguarding procedure, or a formal privacy notice. Employment status and statutory rights depend on the facts and applicable law.

Reporting concerns

Raise employment, conduct, privacy or safeguarding concerns with Learning Hub through learninghubcustomersupport@gmail.com. In an immediate danger or emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service or child-protection authority.

About Us

Learning Hub – Global Education Platform provides personalised online academic support, international curriculum guidance, examination preparation, Quran & Islamic Studies and educational resources for learners worldwide.

Our Background

Our education experience began with on-site academic facilitation and learner support across schools, homes and learning environments.

Since 2019, Learning Hub has expanded online to make structured academic support, curriculum guidance, exam preparation and learning resources accessible to learners in different locations.

We focus on practical, learner-centred support shaped around the learner’s level, curriculum, goals and learning requirements.

Learning Hub Policies & Terms

Clear information about tutoring, payments, resources, cancellations, privacy and professional conduct.

Terms & Conditions

Learning Hub provides online tutoring, international curriculum support, exam preparation, specialist learning support and educational resources. Lessons may be delivered through Google Meet, Zoom, Skype or another agreed online platform.

Fees, lesson duration, booking details and delivery arrangements are communicated before or at the time of purchase or booking. Payments must be made through the approved Learning Hub payment system.

Students and parents must not make payments directly to individual teachers. Teachers must not request or accept payment outside the approved Learning Hub process.

Free resources are provided for personal educational use. Paid or custom resources are supplied according to the timeframe and delivery method stated at purchase. Reselling, redistribution or commercial reproduction without permission is not permitted.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellation or rescheduling requests made at least 12 hours before a lesson may be accommodated where reasonably possible. Requests made less than 12 hours before a lesson may be declined. A late cancellation or no-show may be treated as a confirmed session. Exceptional circumstances are reviewed individually.

Teacher Payment & Professional Conduct

Teachers working through Learning Hub must not request or accept direct payment from students or parents, or encourage customers to bypass the approved payment process. Any unauthorized payment request may be reported to Learning Hub for review and investigation.

Payment Policy

Prices are displayed or communicated before booking or purchase. Payment is made in advance through the approved payment system. A booking or order is confirmed after successful payment. Learning Hub does not store customers' complete card details; payment information is processed by the payment provider.

Resource Delivery

Free resources may be directly downloadable. Paid or custom resources require payment before preparation. The expected delivery timeframe and method will be communicated. Electronic resources may be delivered by email or through an access/download system, and confirmation may be provided.

Privacy

Learning Hub may collect a customer's name, email address, booking or order information, learning requirements and relevant communications as reasonably necessary to provide services, process orders, provide support and improve the platform.

Payment information is processed by the payment provider. Learning Hub does not store complete payment-card details and does not sell personal information.

Support: For policy questions or to report an unauthorized teacher payment request, contact learninghubcustomersupport@gmail.com.

Billing & Invoices

Learning Hub provides a customer billing record for paid bookings and purchases. Depending on the service, the billing record may include:

  • Invoice or receipt number
  • Customer/student name and email
  • Service, lesson, package or resource purchased
  • Teacher, where applicable
  • Lesson hours or quantity
  • Price and currency
  • Payment status: Paid, Pending, Failed or Refunded
  • Payment date
  • Payment method/provider

Payment Confirmation

A booking or order is confirmed after successful payment through the approved Learning Hub payment system. Payment-card information is processed securely by the payment provider; Learning Hub does not store customers' complete card details.

Invoices & Receipts

Where applicable, Learning Hub may provide an electronic invoice or receipt by email or through the customer access/download system. Customers should keep their payment confirmation and invoice for their records.

Questions About a Payment

For billing, payment, refund or invoice questions, contact learninghubcustomersupport@gmail.com.

Learning & Programmes
What subjects and learning support can I book?

Learning Hub supports English, Mathematics, General Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science/ICT, Business Studies, Economics, Accounting, Psychology, Sociology, History, Geography, Environmental Science, Social Studies and primary subjects, together with homework, revision and exam support.

What makes us different: learners are not forced into a generic course. Tell us the subject, curriculum, level, difficulty and goal so support can be structured around the actual need.
How are lessons personalised?

We consider the learner's current level, curriculum or examination board, specific difficulties, goals, pace and schedule before deciding the lesson focus. Parents and learners can also provide requirements and supporting documents so the teacher begins with useful academic context.

How is a teacher selected for my child or programme?

Learning Hub does not operate as a public teacher marketplace. We review the request and match it against subject, curriculum, level and the type of support required.

Teacher selection is based on suitability for the learner's requirement rather than profile popularity.
Can you help a learner who is behind or struggling?

Yes. Support can focus on foundational gaps, catch-up learning, homework organisation, revision, slower-paced explanation and repeated guided practice. We identify what is blocking progress and build from there.

International Curricula
Which international curricula and boards are supported?

Support is organised across International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, IGCSE, GCSE and AS & A Level, subject to the selected subject and level. Give us the exact subject, board, level and examination session where relevant.

Can I request a specific subject, paper, component or syllabus area?

Yes. Requests can focus on a topic, paper, text, assessment skill, internal task, revision area or examination component. Syllabus extracts, school tasks, worksheets or teacher feedback can also be shared where appropriate.

Can Learning Hub replace my school or award a qualification?

No. Learning Hub provides independent tutoring, curriculum support, exam preparation and educational resources. Official registration, examination entry, grading and certification remain with the relevant institution or awarding body.

Exam Preparation
How does IELTS, PTE Academic or TOEFL preparation work?

Preparation can be complete or focused on a particular skill. Lessons can target Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking through task analysis, timed practice, response planning, error review, language development and exam strategy.

Our approach: identify the gap, practise the relevant task, explain why a response succeeds or fails, and use that evidence to decide what comes next.
Can I book only one exam component?

Yes, where offered. A learner who is already strong in other areas can request focused support such as Writing, Speaking, Reading or Listening rather than automatically purchasing complete preparation.

Do you guarantee a band, score or examination result?

No. We can work towards a target through structured preparation, practice and feedback, but external results also depend on the learner's starting level, preparation time, attendance, independent practice and performance on the day.

Can I request intensive or crash preparation?

Yes. Tell us the examination date and time remaining. Short preparation is prioritised around the highest-value gaps, exam technique and practice rather than superficially covering an entire curriculum.

Quran & Islamic Studies
What Quran and Islamic learning pathways are available?

The Quran pathway can progress through Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Fluent Recitation and Hifz/Memorisation, with Quran Translation, Tafseer and Arabic for Quran also available. Islamic learning includes Islamic Studies, Seerah, Hadith, Fiqh, Duas, Salah & Manners.

Can complete beginners and children start from the foundations?

Yes. Beginners can start with letter recognition, sounds and foundational reading through an appropriate pathway such as Noorani Qaida. Children can learn at an age-appropriate pace, and adults beginning or returning can request a suitable starting level.

Do I have to follow every Quran pathway in order?

No. Placement reflects existing ability and goals. A learner who already reads may need Tajweed or fluency; another may specifically want memorisation, translation or Islamic Studies.

Bookings & Payments
How do I book and what happens after I submit?

Use Booking & Billing to choose the service, lesson duration and booking plan, then provide learner/customer details and learning requirements. The summary shows the service, duration, number of lessons, rate and total.

For a stronger match, include level, curriculum/board, subject, goal, preferred schedule and specific difficulty rather than writing only “need a tutor.”
Why are payments handled through Learning Hub?

Centralised billing keeps the booking connected to the Learning Hub service record and gives families a clear route for payment support, cancellations and service concerns. Teachers must not request or accept direct payment from students or parents.

Can I reschedule or cancel a lesson?

Rescheduling, cancellation and any applicable refund follow the current Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy.

Can I send documents explaining what the learner needs?

Yes. The booking area includes optional Supporting Documents for relevant worksheets, school tasks, syllabus information, feedback or other material that helps us understand the requirement.

Learning Resources
What is available in the Free Learning Library?

The Free Learning Library supports learning beyond live lessons with illustrated stories, worksheets, workbooks, reading and writing practice, activities, revision materials, eBooks and teacher-developed learning aids.

What makes Premium Digital Resources different?

Premium resources are individual educational products with a defined learner level, purpose, scope, price and delivery information. They may include structured workbooks, exam-support packs, topic practice, illustrated learning materials, eBooks or customised resources.

Can I request a customised worksheet or resource?

Where custom support is available, tell us the subject, learner level, curriculum, topic, learning objective and type of material required. This lets the resource be built around a real learning purpose rather than as a generic worksheet.

Can Learning Hub resources be copied or resold?

Not unless the applicable licence or written permission allows it. Original Learning Hub materials must not be publicly uploaded, redistributed, resold or commercially reused without permission.

Contact & Support
I am not sure which programme to choose. What should I do?

Use the Contact & Enquire form and tell us the learner's age or level, subject, curriculum/board, current difficulty and goal. You do not need to guess the correct programme first.

What is the difference between an enquiry and a complaint/support request?

Use Contact & Enquire for programmes, curriculum guidance, bookings, resources and general questions. Use Support / Complaints for an existing service concern, technical issue, payment-process concern, complaint or unauthorised teacher payment request.

What information should I include when reporting a problem?

Include the learner/customer name, relevant booking or service, what happened, when it happened and what assistance is required. Do not send passwords, full card details or unnecessary sensitive payment credentials.

Can I share feedback about my Learning Hub experience?

Yes. Use Feedback & Reviews for learner and customer feedback.

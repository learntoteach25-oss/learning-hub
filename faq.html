Learning & Programmes

What subjects and learning support can I book?

Learning Hub brings academic tutoring and practical learning support into one platform. Current areas include English, Mathematics, General Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science/ICT, Business Studies, Economics, Accounting, Psychology, Sociology, History, Geography, Environmental Science, Social Studies and primary-level subjects, together with homework, revision and examination support.

We also support learners who need structured catch-up work, stronger foundations or help organising schoolwork rather than simply another subject lesson.

What is different: you do not have to force your requirement into a generic course. Tell us the learner's subject, curriculum, level and goal; the support can then be structured around that actual need.
How are lessons personalised for an individual learner?

Personalisation starts before the lesson. We consider the learner's current level, curriculum or examination board, specific difficulties, learning goals, preferred pace and available schedule. The teacher can then focus lesson time on the areas that require attention instead of following a one-size-fits-all sequence.

For example, one learner may need concept rebuilding, another examination technique, and another guided practice and confidence with communication. Materials, practice and lesson pacing can be adjusted accordingly.

Parents or learners can add detailed learning requirements—and supporting documents where available—during the booking process so the request reaches us with useful academic context.
How is a teacher selected for my child or programme?

Learning Hub does not ask families to choose blindly from a public marketplace of teacher profiles. We first review the request and match it against relevant factors such as subject, curriculum, learner level and the type of support required.

This allows teacher selection to be based on suitability for the learning request rather than profile popularity alone. Where clarification is needed, Learning Hub can contact the family before finalising the arrangement.

Can you help a learner who is behind, struggling or needs extra support?

Yes. Support can focus on foundational gaps, catch-up learning, homework organisation, revision, slower-paced explanation and repeated guided practice. The purpose is to identify what is preventing progress and build from there rather than simply moving through new content.

Learning Hub is an educational support service, not a diagnostic or clinical service. Where a learner has an identified learning need, families can share appropriate educational information so teaching strategies can be adapted within the scope of tutoring.

International Curricula

Which international curricula and boards can Learning Hub support?

Support is organised across international pathways including International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, IGCSE, GCSE and AS & A Level, subject to the selected subject and level.

The important point is that the board is treated as part of the learning requirement—not just as a label. When you enquire or book, provide the exact subject, board, level and examination session where relevant so support can be aligned more accurately.

Can I request a specific subject, paper, component or syllabus area?

Yes. A request can be narrowed to a particular subject and curriculum, and you can explain the exact area causing difficulty—for example a topic, paper, text, assessment skill, internal task, revision area or examination component.

If you have a syllabus extract, school instructions, worksheet, assessment feedback or other relevant material, include it with the learning requirements where the booking form allows supporting documents.

This gives the assigned educator a clearer starting point and reduces time spent discovering information that the learner already has.
Can Learning Hub replace my school or award an official qualification?

No. Learning Hub provides tutoring, curriculum support, examination preparation and educational resources. It is not a school, examination board, university or degree-awarding institution.

Official registration, coursework rules, examination entry, grading and certification remain the responsibility of the learner, school, examination centre or relevant awarding body.

Exam Preparation

How does IELTS, PTE Academic or TOEFL preparation work?

Preparation can be organised as complete exam support or focused work on a particular skill, depending on the pathway selected. Instead of only giving general English lessons, preparation can target the structure and demands of Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking.

Where appropriate, lessons can include task analysis, timed practice, response planning, error review, language development and examination strategy. A learner preparing for a specific test should provide the test name, target date, current level or previous score if available, and the area requiring the most attention.

Our focus: identify the gap, practise the relevant task, review why an answer succeeds or fails, and use that evidence to guide the next lesson.
Can I book preparation for only one exam component?

Yes, where that option is offered. A learner who is already confident in other areas does not necessarily need a full preparation package. Focused support may be arranged around a component such as Writing, Speaking, Reading or Listening.

If several components are weak, complete preparation is usually more coherent because skills often overlap—for example vocabulary, comprehension, timing and response organisation.

Can you guarantee a particular band, score or grade?

No responsible tutoring service can guarantee an external examination result. Scores depend on the learner's starting point, preparation time, attendance, independent practice, performance on the day and the rules and marking of the examination provider.

Learning Hub's role is to provide structured preparation, targeted practice, feedback and academic guidance. We can work towards a stated target, but we do not present that target as a guaranteed outcome.

I have an exam soon. Can I request intensive or crash preparation?

You can explain the examination date and remaining preparation time in your enquiry. The request can then be reviewed to determine what is realistic within the available period.

Short preparation is normally prioritised around the highest-value gaps, examination technique and practice rather than attempting to cover an entire long-term curriculum superficially.

Quran & Islamic Studies

What Quran and Islamic learning pathways are available?

The Quran learning pathway begins with foundations and can progress through Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Fluent Recitation and Hifz/Memorisation. Learners can also explore Quran Translation, Tafseer and Arabic for Quran.

Islamic learning areas include Islamic Studies, Seerah, Hadith, Fiqh, Duas, Salah & Manners. The aim is to allow families to identify the area they actually want to study rather than treating all Quran and Islamic learning as one generic course.

Can complete beginners and children start from the foundations?

Yes. A learner does not need prior Quran reading ability to enquire. Beginners can start with foundational letter recognition, sounds and reading development through an appropriate pathway such as Noorani Qaida before progressing further.

For children, the pace can be kept age-appropriate and structured around manageable progression. Adults beginning or returning to Quran learning can also request a suitable starting level.

Do I have to follow every Quran pathway in order?

No. The pathway illustrates progression, but placement should reflect the learner's existing ability and goals. Someone who already reads may need Tajweed or fluency work; another learner may specifically request memorisation, translation or Islamic Studies.

Share the learner's current ability when booking so unnecessary repetition can be avoided.

Bookings & Payments

How do I book a lesson and what happens after I submit?

Choose the relevant programme or service and continue to Booking & Billing. Select the lesson duration and booking plan, then provide the learner/customer details and learning requirements.

The booking summary shows the selected service, lesson duration, number of lessons, rate and total. Learning Hub then uses the submitted academic information to process the request and arrange suitable support.

For a better match, include the learner's level, curriculum/board, subject, goals, preferred schedule and any specific difficulty instead of writing only “need a tutor.”
Why are payments handled through Learning Hub rather than directly with teachers?

Centralised billing keeps the booking connected to the Learning Hub service record and provides a clearer route for payment-related support, cancellations, service concerns and administration.

Teachers must not request or accept direct payment from students or parents. If anyone asks you to bypass the approved Learning Hub payment process, use the Support / Complaints form to report it.

Can I reschedule or cancel a booked lesson?

Rescheduling, cancellation and any applicable refund depend on the current service conditions and notice provided. Before confirming a booking, review the Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy.

If a genuine issue arises, contact Learning Hub through the support route with the booking details rather than making a separate arrangement outside the platform.

Can I send documents that explain what the learner needs?

Yes. The booking area includes an optional Supporting Documents section for relevant reference material. This may include a worksheet, school task, syllabus information, feedback or another document that helps explain the academic requirement.

Upload only material you are authorised to share and avoid unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Learning Resources

What can I find in the Free Learning Library?

The Free Learning Library is designed to support learning beyond the live lesson. It can include illustrated stories, worksheets, workbooks, reading and writing practice, activities, revision materials, eBooks and teacher-developed learning aids.

Resources are intended for purposeful learning rather than being a random download collection. Availability can grow and change as new materials are added.

What makes Premium Digital Resources different?

Premium resources are presented as individual educational products with their own scope, learner level, purpose, price and delivery information. Depending on the resource, these may include structured workbooks, examination-support packs, topic-based practice, illustrated learning materials, eBooks or customised resources.

This allows a parent, learner or educator to understand what a resource is for before ordering rather than purchasing an undefined bundle.

Can I request a customised worksheet or learning resource?

Where customised resource support is available, use the relevant premium-resource or enquiry route and describe the subject, learner level, curriculum, topic, learning objective and type of material required. Supporting reference material can be provided where permitted.

A customised request is reviewed according to its scope; it should not be assumed to be the same as an existing downloadable resource.

Can I copy, upload, resell or redistribute Learning Hub resources?

Not unless the applicable licence or written permission expressly allows it. Original Learning Hub materials are supplied for authorised educational use and must not be copied, resold, publicly uploaded, redistributed or commercially reused without permission.

Contact & Support

I am not sure which programme to choose. What should I do?

You do not need to guess. Use the Contact & Enquire form and tell us the learner's age or level, subject, curriculum/board, current difficulty and goal. Learning Hub can then direct the enquiry towards the appropriate programme or support route.

What is the difference between an enquiry and a support/complaint request?

Use Contact & Enquire for programme questions, curriculum guidance, bookings, resources and general enquiries. Use Support / Complaints for an existing service concern, technical issue, payment-process concern, complaint or unauthorised teacher payment request.

Keeping these routes separate helps the request reach the appropriate Learning Hub support path instead of being mixed into a general inbox.

What information should I include when reporting a problem?

Include enough information to identify the issue: the learner/customer name, relevant booking or service, what happened, when it happened and what assistance is required. Do not send passwords, full card details or other unnecessary sensitive payment credentials.

Can I share feedback about my Learning Hub experience?

Yes. The Feedback & Reviews page is available for learner and customer feedback, including written feedback and optional supported media submissions where offered.

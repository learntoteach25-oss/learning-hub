Learning & Programmes

What subjects does Learning Hub offer?
Learning Hub supports core academic subjects including English, Mathematics, Sciences, Business and Social Sciences, Psychology, Environmental Studies, primary subjects, homework support and revision support. Available pathways can be explored under Programmes.
Are lessons personalised?
Yes. Learning needs, academic level, curriculum, goals and schedule are considered when arranging suitable support. Teaching pace and strategies may be adapted to the learner.
Do students choose a teacher from a public teacher list?
No. Learning Hub reviews the learner's requirements and recommends or assigns an appropriate educator according to subject, curriculum and learning needs.
Do you provide learning support as well as subject tutoring?
Yes. Learning Support includes foundational learning, learning differences, catch-up learning, homework support and revision support using appropriate teaching strategies and pacing.

International Curricula

Which international curricula are supported?
Learning Hub provides support across pathways including International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge International (CIE), Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, IGCSE, GCSE and AS & A Level. Open International Boards to explore the available pathways.
Can I request support for a specific subject and board?
Yes. Include the subject, board, level and current learning requirement when making an enquiry or booking so the most suitable support can be identified.

Exam Preparation

Do you offer IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL preparation?
Yes. IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL are available as separate exam-preparation pathways with support for individual test components as well as complete preparation.
Can I book only one exam component?
Yes. Where shown in the exam pathway, learners can select focused preparation such as Reading, Listening, Writing or Speaking instead of a complete preparation programme.
Do you guarantee a particular score or examination result?
No. Learning Hub provides preparation, practice and educational support, but examination scores, grades, admissions or other specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Quran & Islamic Studies

What Quran and Islamic learning pathways are available?
Current pathways include Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Hifz Support, Islamic Studies, and Seerah & Islamic History.
Can beginners join Quran lessons?
Yes. Learners can begin with a pathway appropriate to their current reading level, including foundational Noorani Qaida support.

Bookings, Lessons & Payments

How do I book a lesson?
Choose the relevant learning pathway and continue to the Booking & Billing page. You can also send an enquiry if you need help identifying the right programme.
What lesson durations are available?
The booking system provides 25-minute and 50-minute lesson options, along with the available booking plans shown at the time of booking.
Can I cancel or reschedule a lesson?
Cancellation and rescheduling conditions depend on the notice provided. Please review the current Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy before booking.
Should I pay a teacher directly?
No. Payments must be made through the approved Learning Hub payment process. Teachers must not request or accept direct payment from students or parents.

Learning Resources

Does Learning Hub provide free resources?
Yes. The Free Learning Library contains free educational reading resources, including separate Kids’ Stories and Teens’ Stories collections.
Are premium digital resources available?
Yes. Learning Hub is developing premium digital resources alongside the free library. Availability and delivery information is provided with the relevant resource.
Can Learning Hub resources be resold or redistributed?
Not unless permission or the applicable licence specifically allows it. Original Learning Hub materials are intended for permitted educational use under the website policies.

Contact & Support

How can I make a general enquiry?
Use the dedicated Contact & Enquire form for programme, booking, resource and general questions.
How can I report a problem or complaint?
Use the dedicated Support / Complaints form for service concerns, technical issues, complaints or unauthorised teacher payment requests.
Can I share feedback about my Learning Hub experience?
Yes. The Feedback & Reviews page accepts written feedback and optional audio or video messages.

Still need help?

Send your question directly to Learning Hub and we will guide you to the appropriate service or support route.

Learning & Programmes

What subjects does Learning Hub offer?
Learning Hub supports English, Mathematics, Sciences, Business and Social Sciences, Psychology, Environmental Studies, primary subjects, homework and revision support, together with MBA, BBA, B.Ed and M.Ed academic support. Explore them under Programmes.
Are lessons personalised?
Yes. Learning needs, academic level, curriculum, goals and schedule are considered when arranging suitable support. Teaching pace and strategies may be adapted to the learner.
Do students choose a teacher from a public teacher list?
No. Learning Hub reviews the learner's requirements and recommends or assigns an appropriate educator according to subject, curriculum and learning needs.
Do you provide learning support as well as subject tutoring?
Yes. Learning Support includes foundational learning, learning differences, catch-up learning, homework support and revision support using appropriate teaching strategies and pacing.

International Curricula

Which international curricula are supported?
Learning Hub provides support across pathways including International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge International (CIE), Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, IGCSE, GCSE and AS & A Level. Open Programmes and select the relevant board beneath the subject.
Can I request support for a specific subject and board?
Yes. Select the subject and its curriculum or board before booking, or include the subject, board, level and current learning requirement in your enquiry.

Exam Preparation

Do you offer IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL preparation?
Yes. IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL are available as separate exam-preparation pathways with focused and complete preparation support.
Can I book only one exam component?
Yes. Where shown in the exam pathway, learners can select focused preparation such as Reading, Listening, Writing or Speaking.
Do you guarantee a particular score or examination result?
No. Learning Hub provides preparation, practice and educational support, but examination scores, grades, admissions or other specific outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

Quran & Islamic Studies

What Quran and Islamic learning pathways are available?
Current pathways include Noorani Qaida, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Hifz Support, Islamic Studies, and Seerah & Islamic History.
Can beginners join Quran lessons?
Yes. Learners can begin with a pathway appropriate to their current reading level, including foundational Noorani Qaida support.

Bookings & Payments

How do I book a lesson?
Choose the relevant learning pathway and continue to Booking & Billing. The billing summary shows the selected programme, lesson duration, plan, number of lessons, rate and total in PKR.
Can I cancel or reschedule a lesson?
Cancellation and rescheduling conditions depend on the notice provided. Review the current Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy before booking.
Should I pay a teacher directly?
No. Payments must be made through the approved Learning Hub payment process. Teachers must not request or accept direct payment from students or parents.

Learning Resources

What free learning resources are available?
The Free Learning Library includes quality educational resources designed for reading, writing, practice, revision and independent learning. Resources may include illustrated stories, worksheets, workbooks, practice materials, activities, study-support materials, eBooks and teacher-developed learning aids for different age groups and learning goals.

Are premium digital resources available?
Yes. Premium Digital Resources may include professionally prepared workbooks, structured practice packs, exam-support materials, topic-based learning aids, eBooks, activity resources and other downloadable educational materials. Each premium resource is presented separately with its scope, intended learner level, price and delivery information.
Can Learning Hub resources be resold or redistributed?
No, unless written permission or the applicable licence specifically allows it. Original Learning Hub materials are for authorised educational use and must not be copied, resold, uploaded, redistributed or commercially reused without permission.

Contact & Support

How can I make a general enquiry?
Use the dedicated Contact & Enquire form for programme, booking, curriculum, resource or general questions.
How can I report a problem or complaint?
Use the dedicated Support / Complaints form for service concerns, technical issues, payment-process concerns, complaints or unauthorised teacher payment requests.
Can I share feedback about my Learning Hub experience?
Yes. The Feedback & Reviews page accepts written feedback and optional audio or video messages.

Learning & Programmes

What subjects does Learning Hub offer?
Learning Hub provides structured academic support across English, Mathematics, General Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science/ICT, Business Studies, Economics, Accounting, Psychology, Sociology, History, Geography, Environmental Science, Social Studies and primary subjects, together with homework, revision and exam support. Higher-education academic support is also available for selected MBA, BBA, B.Ed and M.Ed requirements. Programme suitability is considered according to the learner’s level, curriculum and academic goal. Explore the current options under Programmes.
Are lessons personalised?
Yes. Lessons are planned around the learner’s current level, curriculum, strengths, gaps and intended outcomes rather than following a single generic lesson format. Depending on the subject and learner, tutors may use guided explanations, worked examples, visual teaching aids, diagrams, presentations, reading extracts, worksheets, practice questions, model responses, quizzes, past-paper style tasks and targeted revision activities. Pace, questioning and practice can be adjusted as progress becomes clearer.
Do students choose a teacher from a public teacher list?
No. Learning Hub does not use a public teacher marketplace. The learner’s subject, curriculum or examination board, level, learning needs and schedule are reviewed first. A suitable educator can then be recommended or assigned on the basis of relevant teaching profile and subject fit, helping families avoid selecting a tutor only from a photograph or short public profile.
Do you provide learning support as well as subject tutoring?
Yes. Learning Support is intended for learners who may need stronger foundations, catch-up support, homework guidance, revision structure or a different pace of instruction. Support can break complex tasks into manageable stages and use visual prompts, examples, scaffolded practice, repetition, guided questioning and skill-building activities. It complements subject teaching while encouraging confidence and increasingly independent learning.

International Curricula

Which international curricula are supported?
Learning Hub supports major international pathways including International Baccalaureate (IB), Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, OxfordAQA, IGCSE, GCSE and AS & A Level. Teaching is aligned to the learner’s stated curriculum rather than treating similarly named qualifications as identical. Where relevant, support may focus on syllabus content, assessment objectives, command terms, analytical or structured writing, topic practice, examination technique and revision planning. See Programmes for the current subject pathways.
Can I request support for a specific subject and board?
Yes. Please provide the exact subject, board or curriculum, level or grade, current topic and learning goal when enquiring. You may also share relevant syllabus information, assignment instructions, school materials or areas of difficulty where appropriate. This allows Learning Hub to assess the requirement more accurately and match the learner with suitable support.

Exam Preparation

Do you offer IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL preparation?
Yes. IELTS, PTE Academic and TOEFL are treated as dedicated exam-preparation pathways rather than ordinary conversational-English lessons. Preparation can include diagnostic review, exam format and task familiarisation, Reading and Listening strategies, structured Writing practice, Speaking practice, vocabulary and language development, timed tasks, model or guided responses, error analysis, practice materials and exam-style exercises. The emphasis is on understanding the assessment demands, improving weak areas and developing a practical preparation strategy for the learner’s target test.
Can I book only one exam component?
Yes, where that focused pathway is available. A learner who needs help mainly with Writing, Speaking, Reading or Listening does not necessarily need to repeat a complete preparation programme. Component-focused lessons can concentrate on the relevant task types, scoring requirements, common errors, strategy, timed practice and feedback. A broader programme is more appropriate when weaknesses appear across several skills.
Do you guarantee a particular score or examination result?
No responsible education provider can guarantee an external examination score. Learning Hub can provide structured preparation, appropriate teaching aids, targeted practice, feedback and progress-focused support, but the final result also depends on factors such as the learner’s starting level, attendance, independent practice, examination performance and the awarding organisation’s assessment. Any target score is therefore used as a preparation goal, not a guaranteed outcome.

Quran & Islamic Studies

What Quran and Islamic learning pathways are available?
Learning pathways include Noorani Qaida for foundations, Quran Reading, Tajweed, Hifz Support, Islamic Studies, and Seerah & Islamic History. Depending on the pathway and level, lessons may use guided reading, repetition, pronunciation correction, age-appropriate visual material, structured notes, revision prompts, memorisation support and learning activities. The purpose is to provide organised, level-appropriate learning rather than placing every learner into the same course sequence.
Can beginners join Quran lessons?
Yes. Complete beginners can start from an appropriate foundation level, including Noorani Qaida where suitable. The teacher can work progressively on letter recognition, joining and reading skills, pronunciation and fluency before advancing to more demanding reading. Learners who already have some experience can be assessed from their current level rather than automatically starting again from the beginning.

Bookings & Payments

How do I book a lesson?
Start by choosing the relevant subject, programme or learning pathway and provide the learner’s level, curriculum or examination, goals and preferred schedule. Continue to Booking & Billing when applicable. The booking summary is designed to show the selected service, lesson duration, plan, number of lessons, rate and total before the arrangement is finalised. Where teacher matching is required, Learning Hub reviews the learning requirement before assigning or recommending an educator.
Can I view the PKR price in another currency?
Yes. Supported currency displays may be used to help international families understand the approximate value of a PKR-based price. Currency conversion is indicative and can change with exchange rates or payment-provider processing. The booking summary should be checked carefully for the actual payable basis before payment.
Can I cancel or reschedule a lesson?
Yes, subject to the applicable notice period and service conditions. The treatment of a cancellation can differ depending on how much notice is given, so families should review the current Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy rather than relying on an informal arrangement. This keeps scheduling and payment expectations clear for both learners and tutors.
Should I pay a teacher directly?
No. Payments for Learning Hub services must follow the approved Learning Hub payment process. A tutor should not ask a learner or parent to bypass the platform or make an unauthorised direct payment. If you receive such a request, do not send funds; retain the relevant details and report the matter through the Support / Complaints form.

Learning Resources

What free learning resources are available?
The Free Learning Library is designed as a practical learning collection rather than a page of basic downloads. Depending on availability, it can include carefully presented worksheets, workbooks, reading and writing activities, practice exercises, revision and study materials, educational eBooks, activities, Kids’ Stories and Teens’ Stories. Resources may use illustrations, diagrams, guided examples, reflection prompts, vocabulary work, comprehension tasks, practice questions and other teaching aids to make independent or supported learning clearer and more engaging.

Are premium digital resources available?
Yes. Premium Digital Resources are intended to provide more developed learning value than a simple printable sheet. Depending on the product, this may include professionally structured workbooks, illustrated educational material, topic or skill-based practice packs, guided learning activities, reading resources, revision material, eBooks and extended learning products. Product descriptions should identify the content, intended learner or purpose, format, price and delivery information so customers can understand what they are purchasing before payment.
Can Learning Hub resources be resold or redistributed?
Not unless the resource itself or written permission expressly allows it. Learning Hub-created materials may contain original writing, design, illustrations, educational structure and other protected content. Purchasing or downloading a resource does not automatically transfer ownership or commercial redistribution rights. Users should follow the licence or website terms attached to the relevant material.

Contact & Support

How can I make a general enquiry?
Use the Contact & Enquire form for questions about programmes, subjects, curricula, examination preparation, learning support, resources, bookings or general service information. Providing the learner’s level, subject or programme and the specific help required allows the enquiry to be reviewed more efficiently.
How can I report a problem or complaint?
Use the dedicated Support / Complaints form for service concerns, technical problems, booking or payment issues, complaints, or suspected unauthorised payment requests. Include enough detail to identify the issue and, where relevant, supporting information such as the booking context, dates or screenshots. This helps the matter be reviewed accurately rather than handled through an unclear informal message.
Can I share feedback about my Learning Hub experience?
Yes. The Feedback & Reviews page accepts written feedback and, where available, optional audio or video messages. Useful feedback may cover teaching quality, communication, learning materials, platform experience, support and the learner’s overall experience. Feedback helps Learning Hub identify strengths as well as areas that need improvement.

Still Need Help?

If your question is not covered above, send the learner’s subject or service requirement, level or curriculum, and the specific assistance you need. Learning Hub can then direct your enquiry to the appropriate programme, resource, booking or support route instead of giving you a generic response.

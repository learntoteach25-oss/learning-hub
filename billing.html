Customer Billing Records

Learning Hub provides a customer record for paid bookings and purchases. Depending on the service, the billing record may include:

  • Invoice or receipt number
  • Customer/student name and email
  • Service, lesson, package or resource purchased
  • Teacher, where applicable
  • Lesson hours or quantity
  • Price and currency
  • Payment status: Paid, Pending, Failed or Refunded
  • Payment date
  • Payment method/provider

Payment Confirmation

A booking or order is confirmed after successful payment through the approved Learning Hub payment system. Payment-card information is processed securely by the payment provider; Learning Hub does not store customers' complete card details.

Invoices & Receipts

Where applicable, Learning Hub may provide an electronic invoice or receipt by email or through the customer access/download system. Customers should keep their payment confirmation and invoice for their records.

Questions About a Payment

For billing, payment, refund or invoice questions, contact learntoteach25@gmail.com.

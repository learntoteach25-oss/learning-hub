Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery

Our refund, cancellation and delivery policy is designed to provide clear expectations for learners and families.

1

Cancellation 12 Hours or More Before a Class

A learner may cancel a scheduled class at least twelve (12) hours before its start time. The amount paid for that session will be refunded less a fifteen percent (15%) cancellation and payment-processing fee.

2

Cancellation Less Than 12 Hours Before a Class

No refund is available for cancellation less than twelve (12) hours before the scheduled start time. One rescheduling opportunity may be offered, subject to tutor and schedule availability.

3

Cancellation by Learning Hub or the Tutor

If Learning Hub or the assigned tutor cancels a class, the learner may choose a full refund for that session or free rescheduling.

4

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be initiated within seven (7) business days. The time for funds to appear may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

5

Premium Digital Resources

Premium digital resources are non-refundable after delivery or access unless the supplied resource is defective, corrupted or inaccessible. A working replacement or access will be provided, or a refund where the problem cannot reasonably be resolved.

6

Digital Delivery

Premium digital resources will be delivered within seven (7) days after payment confirmation unless a different delivery time is clearly stated before purchase.

7

Requests

Cancellation, rescheduling and refund requests may be submitted through the Support / Complaints form.

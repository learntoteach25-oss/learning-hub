Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery

Clear expectations for learners and families regarding cancellations, refunds and delivery.

1

Cancellation 12 Hours or More Before a Class

A learner may cancel at least twelve (12) hours before the start time. The session payment will be refunded less a fifteen percent (15%) cancellation and payment-processing fee.

2

Cancellation Less Than 12 Hours Before a Class

No refund is available. One rescheduling opportunity may be offered, subject to tutor and schedule availability.

3

Cancellation by Learning Hub or the Tutor

The learner may choose a full refund for that session or free rescheduling.

4

Approved refunds will be initiated within seven (7) business days. Posting time may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

5

Premium Digital Resources

Premium digital resources are non-refundable after delivery or access unless defective, corrupted or inaccessible. A working replacement or refund will be provided where the problem cannot reasonably be resolved.

6

Digital Delivery

Premium digital resources will be delivered within seven (7) days after payment confirmation unless a different time is stated before purchase.

Requests

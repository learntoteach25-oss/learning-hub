Quran & Islamic learning

Structured Quran and Islamic studies classes.

Dedicated online classes for Hifz, Nazra, Qirat, Tajweed and Islamic studies, taught with careful pacing, correct pronunciation and respectful, structured guidance.

Hifz-ul-Quran

Guided memorisation with daily sabaq, sabqi and manzil revision cycles to keep memorised portions strong.

Nazra-e-Quran

Fluent, correct reading of the Quran from the Mushaf, building accuracy before memorisation or advanced recitation.

Qirat

The art of melodious, rule-correct recitation, developed through guided listening and repeated practice.

Tajweed

The rules that govern correct pronunciation and articulation of Quranic letters and sounds.

Islamic Studies

Core Islamic knowledge including Seerah, basic Fiqh, Duas and Akhlaq, taught in an age-appropriate way.

Arabic Reading & Language

Arabic letter recognition, reading practice and foundational vocabulary to support Quranic understanding.

Research-informed teaching

How Learning Hub turns evidence into better learning.

Lessons are structured around well-established learning principles, then adapted to the learner, subject, age, curriculum and assessment requirements.

Retrieval & Practice

Low-stakes recall, guided questioning and deliberate practice help learners strengthen access to knowledge.

Research-informed: retrieval-practice literature including Roediger & Karpicke.

Spaced Learning

Important knowledge and skills are revisited over time rather than relying on one intensive study session.

Research-informed: spacing research including Cepeda and colleagues.

Cognitive Load

Complex content is broken into manageable steps, worked examples and guided practice before independent application.

Research-informed: Cognitive Load Theory associated with John Sweller and subsequent research.

Dual Coding & Visualisation

Words, diagrams, models and visual organisers are combined when they improve understanding and recall.

Research-informed: dual-coding work associated with Allan Paivio and later instructional research.

Formative Assessment

Frequent checks for understanding, feedback and error analysis are used to adjust instruction.

Research-informed: formative-assessment research associated with Black & Wiliam.

Metacognition

Learners plan, monitor and evaluate their approach so they gradually become more independent.

Research-informed: metacognitive strategy research and classroom evidence reviews.

Explicit Instruction

New concepts are modelled clearly, practised with support and then transferred to independent tasks.

Research-informed: explicit-instruction principles including Rosenshine's work.

Communicative Learning

For languages, students use purposeful speaking, listening, reading and writing tasks rather than isolated drills alone.

Applied alongside curriculum objectives, learner needs and assessment criteria.

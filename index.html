Study Skills4 min read

How to Make Online Learning More Effective

Create a focused routine, participate actively and turn online lessons into measurable learning progress.

Read article
Effective online learning needs a clear goal for every session. Prepare before the lesson, keep distractions low, ask questions, practise during the lesson and finish with a short review. Small, consistent routines usually create stronger progress than occasional long study sessions.

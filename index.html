Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains cancellation, rescheduling, refunds and delivery arrangements for Learning Hub Tutoring's online lessons, plans and premium digital educational resources.

2. Student Cancellation — 12 Hours or More Before Class

If a student cancels a scheduled lesson at least 12 hours before the scheduled start time, the eligible refund will be processed less a 15% cancellation and payment-processing fee.

3. Late Cancellation — Less Than 12 Hours

Cancellations made less than 12 hours before the scheduled lesson are not eligible for a refund. Learning Hub may provide one rescheduling opportunity, subject to tutor availability.

4. Cancellation by Learning Hub or Tutor

If Learning Hub or the assigned tutor cancels a confirmed lesson and a suitable replacement time cannot be arranged, the customer may choose a full refund for the affected lesson or free rescheduling.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are normally processed within 7 business days. The time for the refunded amount to appear in the customer's account may also depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

6. Premium Digital Resources

Premium digital resources are non-refundable after delivery or access has been provided, except where the resource is defective, corrupted or materially inaccessible.

7. Defective, Corrupted or Inaccessible Resources

If a paid digital resource is defective, corrupted or inaccessible, Learning Hub will make reasonable efforts to provide a working replacement or restore access. If the issue cannot reasonably be resolved, a full refund may be provided for the affected resource.

8. Digital Resource Delivery

Premium digital resources are normally delivered within 7 days after payment confirmation and verification. Delivery may be by email, secure access link, download or another electronic method stated for the resource.

9. Student Portal and Service Access

Where a Student Portal is applicable, access is activated after the relevant payment has been confirmed and verified. Portal information may include teacher details, lesson timing, links and other service information.

10. Refund Requests and Customer Support

Refund or cancellation requests should be sent to learninghubcustomersupport@gmail.com with the customer's name, booking/order details and reason for the request. General and booking enquiries may be sent to hublearning117@gmail.com.
